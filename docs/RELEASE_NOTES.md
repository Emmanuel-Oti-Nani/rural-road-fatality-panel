Release v1.0.0 - U.S. Rural Road Fatality County Panel, 2015-2024

First public release.
- County-by-year panel: 3,143 counties x 2015-2024 (31,430 rows); deaths and crashes split rural/urban (2020 Census map and FARS label), by person type, road class, and road owner; population; 2024 public road miles by class and owner.
- Crash crosswalk: 358,460 fatal crashes linked to 2020 counties, urban areas, places, and county subdivisions, with match flags.
- Long table of deaths by county, year, location, road class, owner, and person type.
- Codebook, join rules, provenance log, QA report, limitations.
- Validation tables: FARS rural/urban label vs 2020 Census urban areas.
- Rural Road Safety Atlas (web map).
Data: FARS 2015-2023 Final Files, 2024 Annual Report File. 2024 will be updated to the Final File in v1.0.1.
